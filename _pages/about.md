I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science in the Information Systems and Analytics Department at Bryant University. I also serve as an AI Fellow and on the Honors Advisory Council. An ACUE certified instructor, I primarily teach Information Systems and Analytics (ISA) and Applied Analytics (AA) classes. 

My research interests include affective computing, digital phenotyping, health informatics, mobile health, natural language processing, human-computer interaction, accessibility, and sustainability. My recent research on wearable technology for affective computing is supported by the [Rhode Island Institutional Development Award (IDeA) Network of Biomedical Research Excellence](https://ror.org/05y9ek347) from the National Institute of General Medical Sciences of the National Institutes of Health under grant number P20GM103430. 

I completed my Data Science PhD from Worcester Polytechnic Institute in Spring 2022. My dissertation research is part of the [Emutivo](https://emutivo.wpi.edu) project on digital mental health screening. I was primarily supported through the [Graduate Assistance in Areas of National Need](https://www.wpi.edu/student-experience/resources/academic-advising/fellowships-scholarships/graduate-assistance-areas) Fellowship in Computer Science to Support Data-Driven Computing Research. 

I enjoy writing, solving puzzles, and walking with my dog Bumper.
